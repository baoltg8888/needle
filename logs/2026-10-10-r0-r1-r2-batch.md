## Interpretation
1. **Every reward beats B0 by ~18–26 EM points.** Most of the gain is from learning the protocol (no invented citations, valid format), which all three rewards enforce via the −1 invalid penalty.
2. **Answer accuracy: R0 ≈ R1.** The difference is within noise for both seeds (H1 confirmed).
3. **R0 collapses into shortcuts, two different ways depending on seed (H2/H3 confirmed, strongly).**
   - Seed 8: the policy learned to search **once** and answer with **no citations at all** (`ANSWER[Animorphs]`). The environment accepts an answer without citations as valid, and EM-only reward does not care, so citation precision and evidence coverage drop to 0. Training curves show it: citation precision 0.13 at steps 65–96, 0.00 from step 97 on; searches 1.3 → 1.0.
   - Seed 7: the opposite degenerate habit. It always uses all 3 searches, mostly repeating the same query (229/278 episodes have a duplicate), because there is no cost. Citation precision is the lowest of any trained run (0.62).
   - This matches CaRR: outcome-only RL gets the answer right without showing (or using) evidence.
4. **R1 is the most stable**: identical EM across seeds (42.7%), highest citation precision (0.73–0.75), few duplicate searches.
5. **R2 vs R1 is inconclusive**: seed 8 is +6.0 pts (CI excludes 0), seed 7 is −1.7 (CI includes 0). Mean +2.1. Retrieval recall is slightly higher for R2 in both seeds (0.88–0.89 vs 0.84–0.85), consistent with H4 (it searches a bit better when unsure), but with 2 seeds this is suggestive only.
6. Seed variance is up to ~8 EM points (R2), larger than the ±2–4 expected (H5 partly wrong); differences under ~6 pts between methods should not be claimed.

## Thesis mapping
- RQ1 (does GRPO help?): yes, +18 to +26 pts for every reward.
- RQ2 (does the evidence reward matter?): answer accuracy no, grounding yes. Without it, the agent can stop citing entirely (R0 s8).
- RQ3 (search behaviour): with no search cost, behaviour is unstable (1 search vs. 3 duplicate searches). Penalties keep it at ~2 distinct searches. Gating the penalty has a small, uncertain effect.
